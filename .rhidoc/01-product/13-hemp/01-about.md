# About

Hemp is a Rust-specific Luminous app for understanding software and directing
its change through visual tools.

## North star

> when we have succeeded, an engineer will never have to look at source code again

Hemp supplies what an engineer needs from reading source after subtracting the
work delegated to an AI coding agent. The engineer retains judgment over
purpose, vocabulary, composition, and acceptable behavior. Agents maintain
implementation and the evidence that supports those decisions.

The design admits extensive metaprogramming, generated instrumentation, and
annotations whose manual maintenance is impractical. Generated explanations
remain accountable to source and execution evidence.

The static inspector (doc01.13.03) presents crates, modules, documentation,
and dependencies from a pipeline-produced document. Execution observations
constitute a separate layer over those source identities.
