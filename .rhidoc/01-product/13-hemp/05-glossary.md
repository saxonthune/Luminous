# Glossary

- **HEMPTERM1.** [p] **crate-container** — a picture-in-picture node containing a crate's modules.

- **HEMPTERM2.** [p] **soldered PCB principle** — semantic flatness when zoomed in: nothing to expand, hide, or move around.
